Emergency Preparedness Training

Prepare your household for typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires, and power outages.

What you’ll take away
Create a personal or family emergency plan and an essential supplies checklist.

Weekly modules
  • Personal and Family Emergency Preparedness

    Assess risks, plan for emergencies, and assemble a go-bag and contact list.

  • Disaster Survival and Safety

    Understand evacuation and sheltering for earthquakes, typhoons, floods, and fires.

  • First Aid and Emergency Response Awareness

    Learn first-aid and CPR awareness, bleeding response, and emergency communication.

  • Community Resilience and Recovery

    Plan for safe water, sanitation, outages, and post-disaster recovery.

Financial Literacy Training

Learn to budget, save, manage debt, and make informed decisions about long-term financial goals.

What you’ll take away
Prepare a budget, savings target, financial roadmap, and a debt-management plan if needed.

Weekly modules
  • Money Management and Budgeting

    Allocate income, track expenses, and distinguish needs from wants.

  • Savings and Emergency Funds

    Set savings goals and plan for predictable and emergency expenses.

  • Debt Management and Financial Protection

    Explore debt repayment, responsible borrowing, scam awareness, and insurance basics.

  • Long-Term Financial Planning

    Understand compounding, investment risk, and retirement planning.

Urban Gardening

Grow vegetables and herbs in containers, vertical gardens, or available backyard space.

What you’ll take away
Start a small vegetable or herb garden suited to the space you have.

Weekly modules
  • Getting Started with Urban Gardening

    Assess space, sunlight, location, and container options.

  • Seeds, Soil, and Plant Nutrition

    Explore seed starting, growing media, compost, and watering.

  • Plant Care and Pest Management

    Recognize common pests and learn ways to maintain plant health.

  • Harvesting and Sustainable Food Production

    Explore harvesting, succession planting, cost tracking, and basic selling.

Hydroponics and Biofloc Aquaculture

Explore two technical tracks covering soilless vegetable production and biofloc fish production.

What you’ll take away
Understand the equipment and monitoring each production system requires.

Learning tracks
  • Hydroponics

    Explore system types, nutrient solutions, pH and electrical conductivity, seedling care, troubleshooting, and production costs.

  • Biofloc Aquaculture

    Learn about aeration, stocking density, water-quality monitoring, and contingency planning. The detailed module list will be posted when finalized.

Basic Virtual Assistant Training

Build digital skills, communication habits, and job-application skills for entry-level remote work.

What you’ll take away
Develop foundational skills to pursue entry-level virtual assistant opportunities.
