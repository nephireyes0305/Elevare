How registration works

  1. Get the official GCash or bank transfer details from your Elevare ambassador and pay ₱1,800.
  2. Send your proof of payment directly to your ambassador.
  3. Your ambassador coordinates payment verification with Elevare’s admins. Once approved, you’ll receive a registration code.
  4. Use the code to complete your registration. After signing in, open My Trainings to access your two courses.

The ₱200 CareLink Community contribution is separate from Elevare training enrollment.
